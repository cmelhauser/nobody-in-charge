Pagano, M. E., Friend, K. B., Tonigan, J. S., & Stout, R. L. (2004). Helping other alcoholics in Alcoholics Anonymous and drinking outcomes: Findings from Project MATCH. *Journal of Studies on Alcohol*, 65(6), 766-773. https://doi.org/10.15288/jsa.2004.65.766

Author manuscript full text: PMCID PMC3008319, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3008319/

Summary: A prospective Project MATCH analysis that found AA-related helping was associated with lower relapse risk in the year after treatment, independent of meeting attendance. Interpret it as an observational association in its study context, not proof of universal causation.
