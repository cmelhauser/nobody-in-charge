# Citation

**Alcoholics Anonymous: The Story of How More Than One Hundred Men Have Recovered from
Alcoholism.** First edition, 1939. New York: Works Publishing.

**What is held is a reprint, not the 1939 printing.** The file is a 1999 publication by BBSG whose
own title page reads "The 4th Edition of Alcoholics Anonymous", where "4th edition" refers to that
reprint's own numbering rather than to AAWS's fourth edition of the Big Book. It states that it
"contains a complete reprint of the 1st edition 1939" and asserts "No Copyright. No rights
reserved."

Describe it as the 1999 BBSG reprint of the 1939 first-edition text. Calling it "the 1939 first
edition" would misdescribe the artifact.
