# Summary

The 1939 first edition of *Alcoholics Anonymous*, held as a 1999 third-party reprint. 193 pages in
this printing, about 113,000 words: the foreword, the Doctor's Opinion, the twelve chapters of
Bill W.'s exposition including the Twelve Steps, and the original personal stories.

**First used as claim support on 17 August 2026**, in appendix A13. The thirty personal
stories were read in full and coded against one modelling choice, the arrival term of A2.7, which
gives member-carried arrival the dominant role. The stories support that architecture: personal
contact by a recovered alcoholic, usually a visit and often several, is the recurring shape, and the
one account of arrival without a member, "Lone Endeavor", is framed by the fellowship as its own
untested experiment, in which it records having begun to think the book "inadequate without personal
contact". Read beside the Recovery Dharma census in A12.5, the pair bounds the arrival weighting to
a fellowship and a period rather than to mutual-aid groups in general.

Chapters 4, 9, 15 and 18 cite it, as does Appendix A13; Chapter 4's printed wording (printed pp. 71
and 95) was checked against it on 13 September 2026. Part One and Chapter 5 still source the 1939
book's status through Maxwell, Kurtz and the 1946 Grapevine article rather than through the book
itself, and the book does not contain the Traditions. Anything drawn from it should be quoted
against a page image and the reprint provenance stated, since this artifact is a reprint and its
pagination is not the 1939 printing's.

**A naming trap, recorded because it caught this project.** A second copy of this work was supplied
on 17 August 2026 as "the 4th edition of Alcoholics Anonymous". It is not. The title page reads "The
4th Edition of Alcoholics Anonymous" and, below it, "This book contains a complete reprint of the
1st edition 1939": the fourth edition is the reprinter's own printing number. AA's fourth edition of
2001 has a different set of stories and roughly four hundred more pages; it is held separately as
`BigBook_2001` (a git-ignored copy since 13 September 2026). Anyone offered this file under that name should check the title page and the story titles,
which are the 1939 ones.

**Rights.** See `metadata.json`. The public-domain status of the 1939 text is widely asserted and
has been contested; the document is git-ignored regardless, as every source in this corpus is.
