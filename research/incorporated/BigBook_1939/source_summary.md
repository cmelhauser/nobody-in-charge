# Summary

The 1939 first edition of *Alcoholics Anonymous*, held as a 1999 third-party reprint. 193 pages in
this printing, about 113,000 words: the foreword, the Doctor's Opinion, the twelve chapters of
Bill W.'s exposition including the Twelve Steps, and the original personal stories.

**Not yet used as claim support.** No chapter currently cites it. It is held because Part One and
Chapter 5 discuss the 1939 book as the movement's first written record of its experience, a claim
presently sourced through Maxwell, Kurtz and the 1946 Grapevine article rather than through the
book itself. Anything drawn from it in future should be quoted against a page image and the reprint
provenance stated, since this artifact is a reprint and its pagination is not the 1939 printing's.

**Rights.** See `metadata.json`. The public-domain status of the 1939 text is widely asserted and
has been contested; the document is git-ignored regardless, as every source in this corpus is.
