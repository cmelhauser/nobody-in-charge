# Summary

Contemporary annual report of the American Temperance Union. Use as primary evidence for the organization’s stated activities, membership language, reform strategy, and contemporaneous discussion of temperance. It is particularly useful for tracing the institutional context in which Washingtonian societies operated.

Limitations: a report is an organizational self-presentation, not a neutral account; corroborate broad claims with other primary records. The included OCR is from the archive’s DjVu text and requires page-image verification.
