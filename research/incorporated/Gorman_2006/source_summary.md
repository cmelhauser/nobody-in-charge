# Summary

A preliminary agent-based model of drinking. Agents move on a one-dimensional lattice and drink
under simple local rules of social contagion, and a single bar acts as an attractor that clusters
drinkers. The authors use it to argue that agent-based simulation can represent how drinking
behaviour spreads and how an environmental feature changes its distribution.

**Read in full at PubMed Central on 14 September 2026**, and held from 26 October 2026 as the PDF
the Human Author saved in a browser (6 pages).

What it supports here is background in the paper's survey of dynamical and agent-based models of
drinking. The model has neither outlet density nor groups in the sense this project uses, and
nothing in this project is calibrated to it.
