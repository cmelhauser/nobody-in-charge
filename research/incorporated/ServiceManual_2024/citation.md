# Citation

Alcoholics Anonymous World Services. *The A.A. Service Manual Combined with Twelve Concepts for
World Service by Bill W.* 2024-2026 edition, revised and updated (BM-31). New York: A.A. World
Services, 2024. Online edition posted by A.A.W.S.

The Twelve Concepts and their essays are Bill W.'s, adopted by the General Service Conference in
1962. Cite them as W., Bill, *Twelve Concepts for World Service* (1962), by Concept number, with
this edition's page, which in the Concepts section runs C-1 onward.
