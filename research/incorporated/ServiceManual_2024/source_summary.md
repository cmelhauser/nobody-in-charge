# Source summary: The A.A. Service Manual with the Twelve Concepts (2024-2026 edition)

**Read in part on 13 September 2026**: Concepts IV and V in full, Concept XI on rotation, and every
passage in the manual that mentions rotation. Held git-ignored, at the Human Author's direction.

## Why it was wanted

`HANDOFF.md` section 10 carried the essay accompanying Concept 4 as the one place a rule about the
size of a rotating pool might exist, and assumed it had to be bought. AAWS posts the whole manual
free on its own file host.

## What it says

- **Concept IV has no pool-size rule.** Its essay is about voting participation in proportion to
  responsibility, and about the early headquarters' trouble when the trustees held all the
  authority and the office all the responsibility.
- **Rotation lives in Concept XI**, which ties term length to responsibility and adopted staff
  rotation after a single dominant worker collapsed, then warns against attempting more rotation
  than that.
- **Concept V defends minorities** as the protection against a hasty or uninformed majority, and
  describes a two-thirds rule and lots drawn between majority and minority candidates.

## What it is not

A prescription for the world service structure written in 1962, not evidence about how groups
behave, and not a home-group rulebook.
