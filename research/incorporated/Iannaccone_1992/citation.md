# Citation

**Iannaccone, L. R. (1992). "Sacrifice and Stigma: Reducing Free-Riding in Cults, Communes, and
Other Collectives." *Journal of Political Economy* 100(2): 271-291. doi:10.1086/261818.**

The club-good account of costly group requirements: sacrifice and stigma screen out prospective
members whose participation would be low and raise participation among those who join. Chapter 2
uses it for the Sons of Temperance; Chapters 12 and 15 for the diminishing returns behind this
book's participatory resources.

The citation is authoritative in `research/SOURCES.md`, which also keeps the read status.
