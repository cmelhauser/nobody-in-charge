# Source summary: The Twelve Concepts for World Service

**Read in full on 17 August 2026** from the official aa.org posting of the short form. Listed in
`HANDOFF.md` as unread, and as the place where the fellowship's own thinking about rotation is
set out at greatest length.

## Why it was wanted

Chapter 10 makes one of the book's three headline claims: a rotating pool must scale with the
group, as a fraction rather than a headcount, or influence concentrates without limit. The
chapter says this is something the model knows and A.A. does not say. That claim had been checked
against Tradition 9 and the 1953 commentary but not against the Concepts, which is where an
answer would most plausibly have been.

## The finding

**It is not there, and the near miss is informative.** Concept 4 establishes a Right of
Participation, asking for voting representation in reasonable proportion to the responsibility
each element of the structure discharges. A.A. therefore does hold a proportionality principle
about service, explicitly and in its own words.

But it proportions a different pair. Concept 4 proportions *voting weight to responsibility
discharged*. The model's result concerns *the size of the rotating pool relative to the size of
the group being served*. A structure could satisfy Concept 4 exactly, every service body weighted
precisely to its responsibility, and still rotate twelve people through a fellowship of eight
hundred, which is the configuration Chapter 10 shows to be a permanent oligarchy.

Concept 9 is the other one that touches this and points elsewhere: it says primary world service
leadership passed from the founders to the trustees, a claim about succession rather than about
breadth.

The chapter's claim survives in a sharpened form. A.A. has the instinct for proportionality and
applies it to representation rather than to the rotating pool. That is a more interesting
statement than "A.A. does not say", and it is what the source actually supports.

## What was not read

The long-form Concepts and the essays accompanying them in the A.A. Service Manual. The short
form states each Concept in full; the essays elaborate. If a size rule exists anywhere it would
most likely be in the Concept 4 essay, and that remains unchecked.
