# Summary

An identification result for nonlinear errors-in-variables models with continuously distributed
variables and nonclassical measurement error, using an instrument or a repeated measurement where
earlier methods needed validation data. The key assumption is that some measure of location of the
error distribution, its mean, mode or median, is zero conditional on the true regressor, and
identification rests on an eigenvalue-eigenfunction decomposition of an integral operator
(abstract and introduction, 195 to 197).

**Read in part on 13 September 2026:** the abstract, the introduction and the assumptions of
section 2 (195 to 200). The copy is the published article as downloaded from JSTOR, cover page
included, which the first author posts; the cover page carries a download stamp from February 2024
that is not this project's. Chapter 13 and the paper cite it, with Schennach (2004), for what a real
identification design would have to solve, and Chapter 13 says both are known through their
application in Cunha, Heckman and Schennach, who do draw on them (their page 3). That description
remains accurate for everything the book says.
