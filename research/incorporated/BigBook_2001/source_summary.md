# Source summary: Alcoholics Anonymous, fourth edition (2001)

**Read in part on 17 August 2026.** Title page, copyright page, contents and all three parts of
the personal stories. The programme chapters were not read for this purpose; they are unchanged
from 1939 and are covered by `BigBook_1939`.

## Why it was wanted

Appendix A13 counted, in the 1939 first edition, how many of the twenty-six story segments describe
a person arriving because a recovered alcoholic reached them. Twenty of twenty-six did. The open
item was that the fourth edition carries a different set of stories, so the finding was a reading
of 1939 and could not be extended to the fellowship as it is now.

## The census

Forty-two stories: ten Pioneers, seventeen They Stopped in Time, fifteen They Lost Nearly All.
Thirty state the initiating channel plainly.

| Initiating channel | Stories |
|---|---:|
| A member sought the subject out | 13 |
| The subject contacted the fellowship | 6 |
| A professional or an institution referred | 11 |
| Not stated plainly enough to code | 12 |

The twelve are reported as unresolved rather than assigned, on the principle the release gate
applies to an interval crossing zero.

## What it shows

The member-initiated share is 43 per cent, against 77 per cent in 1939. The middle category, a
subject who goes looking and finds the fellowship, could not have existed in 1939: it was small,
unadvertised and not in any directory. The third category is larger still, and the stories name
judges, colleges, counsellors and treatment centres.

That supports the model's two-term arrival structure, and specifically supports the exogenous term
whose code comment names courts, treatment and desperation. In 1939 that term had almost nothing to
point at.

It also bounds the model. `lam_exog` is fixed at 0.12 per week for every run, so the model cannot
represent a fellowship whose exogenous arrival grew with its own institutional presence. The two
censuses together are the clearest evidence in the appendix that this is a limitation rather than a
detail.

## What it is not

Not a sample. The stories are selected by the fellowship for publication, and selection on outcome
is total: every subject recovered. Nothing here estimates the arrival mix in any population, and
the comparison between editions is a comparison between two edited collections sixty-two years
apart.
