# Summary

A review of why relapse is hard to define and model. The authors argue that treating relapse as a
single event hides a dynamic process, lay out the operational definitions of relapse used across
studies, and describe a nonlinear dynamic systems account of relapse (including a cusp catastrophe
model), with high-risk situations and self-efficacy among the variables that move a person's
trajectory.

**Read in full at PubMed Central on 14 September 2026** as the author manuscript, and held from 26
October 2026 as the PDF the Human Author saved (22 pages). Its pagination is the manuscript's,
not the journal's 724 to 738.

What it supports here is relapse-dynamics background in the paper: the idea that post-treatment
drinking follows a nonlinear dynamic path, which motivates the double-well literature the project
cites. Nothing in the model is calibrated to it.
