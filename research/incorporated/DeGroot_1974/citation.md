# Citation

**DeGroot, M. H. (1974). "Reaching a Consensus." *Journal of the American Statistical Association*
69(345): 118-121.**

The updating model behind Part Two: each member holds a belief, weights the beliefs of others, and
revises to the weighted average, so consensus is the left stationary vector of the trust matrix.
Golub and Jackson (2010) build their result on this model.
