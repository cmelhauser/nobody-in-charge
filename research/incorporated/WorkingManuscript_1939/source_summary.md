# Source summary: the 1939 working manuscript (Hazelden facsimile, 2010)

**Read in full on 12 September 2026**, every facsimile page, from the Human Author's photographs of their own copy.

## What it is

In mid-February 1939 the New York and Akron members had their draft book run off by multilith, a
small offset process, and sent it to members, doctors, ministers and others for comment. The
comments were then collated onto one copy. That copy, with its pencil changes in several hands,
its marginal arguments and its printer's marks, is what this facsimile reproduces. It sits between
the circulated draft and the book published on 10 April 1939, so it shows both what the fellowship
first proposed and who argued with it.

## What it supports

- The circulated text of the Steps and programme chapters, and which changes were made before
  print. Several are sharper than the usual accounts: Step 3's qualifier is already typed, Step
  11's is written in by hand, and the "choose your own conception of God" episode in Bill's Story
  is a handwritten insert that the circulated draft did not contain.
- A consistent editorial direction: second-person instruction recast as first-person report,
  collective "group" language taken out, deference to doctors and ministers, and selling language
  removed.
- The Foreword's statements of anonymity, of not being an organization, of no fees or dues and of
  a single requirement for membership, all already typed in February 1939, beside a typed plan for
  a central foundation with a guaranteed non-alcoholic majority.
- Early-1939 meeting sizes as members reported them, mostly between thirty and eighty.

## What it is not

Not an attribution of hands, and not a dated record: an annotation shows that somebody proposed a
change, not when or with what authority. Hazelden's essays are secondary and anonymous. The OCR
text held here is a search aid.

The full account of the edits, with page references and suggested uses in the manuscript, is in
`edits_and_suggested_uses.md` in this directory.
