# Citation

*The Book That Started It All: The Original Working Manuscript of Alcoholics Anonymous.* Center
City, Minn.: Hazelden, 2010. ISBN 9781592859474 (hardback); 9781616490843 (leather bound). First
printing September 2010. A colour facsimile of the annotated 1939 multilith copy of *Alcoholics
Anonymous*, with two introductory essays, a thematic listing of edits, a transcript of Wilson's
1954 talk on how the book was written, and a short publishing history. The essayists are
anonymous members of AA.

Cite the manuscript layer as "working MS p. N", using the manuscript page number Hazelden prints
beside each facsimile, and Hazelden's own essays as "Hazelden 2010, p. N".
