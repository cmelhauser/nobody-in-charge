# Citation

**Wilson, W. ("By Bill") (1946). "Twelve Suggested Points for A. A. Tradition." *A.A. Grapevine*,
April 1946.**

The article carries two names for the same text, which explains a citation conflict Chapter 5 had
recorded as unresolvable. The Grapevine headline is "Twelve Suggested Points for A. A. Tradition."
Within the body, Bill introduces the list under his own heading: "An Alcoholics Anonymous Tradition
of Relations - Twelve Points to Assure Our Future." Kurtz cites the second; AAWS's pamphlet P-17
also calls the text the "Twelve Points to Assure Our Future." Both names are correct and refer to
this article.

**Still unresolved.** Kurtz cites the April 1946 publication twice with different issue numbers,
2:10 at pages 7-9 and 2:11 at pages 2-3. The scan held here carries no issue or page markers, so it
cannot adjudicate that conflict. Chapter 5 continues to record it as open.
