# Summary

A Cochrane systematic review of Alcoholics Anonymous and clinician-delivered twelve-step
facilitation (TSF) for alcohol use disorder. It pools randomized and quasi-randomized trials and
non-randomized studies, comparing manualized AA/TSF with other treatments across outcomes
including proportion completely abstinent, percentage days abstinent, drinks per drinking day and
alcohol-related consequences. Its conclusions, as the review states them, are that manualized
AA/TSF usually produced higher rates of continuous abstinence than the other established
treatments and that non-manualized AA/TSF may perform as well as them.

and risk-of-bias tables, the forest plots, the search strategies and the references, and held from
