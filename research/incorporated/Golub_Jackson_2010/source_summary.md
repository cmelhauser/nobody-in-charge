# Summary

Formal network-learning paper. In its stated large-network model, learning converges to truth if and only if the influence of the most influential agent vanishes as the society grows. It is the core source for any claim about a formal condition for reliable deliberation.

Use carefully: the result assumes a specific repeated weighted-averaging learning model and independent noisy signals. The book must supply, rather than assume, the correspondence between those conditions and AA's organizational practices.
