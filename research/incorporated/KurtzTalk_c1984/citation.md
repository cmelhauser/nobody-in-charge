# Citation

Kurtz, Ernest. "A Talk About the History of Alcoholics Anonymous From the Letters of Bill Wilson."
Undated recorded talk, narrated by Kurtz. Transcript restored and remastered by Thomas LaPerriere,
historyofrecovery.com; titles and credits narrated by Abel Friend.

**The year is inferred, not stated.** Kurtz says his published answer to the closing question
"does appear on pages 248 and 249" of a book written "five years ago". *Not-God* was published in
1979, which places the talk about 1984. He also refers to a slip he made "three years ago, at the
Rutgers summer school". Nothing in the recording states a date, so the directory is named
`KurtzTalk_c1984` and every citation should carry the "about" rather than assert the year.

This is a distinct item from Kurtz (1991), which is catalogued separately at
`research/incorporated/Kurtz_1991/`.
