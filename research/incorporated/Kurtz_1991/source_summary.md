# Summary

The standard history of Alcoholics Anonymous. Used for organizational history and for the
development of the Traditions.

**Held as a verification index only.** The work is in copyright and was never stored as full
text in this repository. The index carries its vocabulary, which is enough for
`tools/check_book.py` to confirm that a cited subject appears in the work, and is not a
redistributable copy. Any quotation must be checked against a lawfully obtained edition.
